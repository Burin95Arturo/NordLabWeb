ABOUT NORDLAB

Ideas into real objects.

NordLab began in 2020 as a small space for exploring 3D design and making. Over the years, we have continued to grow by incorporating new technologies, techniques and tools, turning ideas into real objects and taking on an ever-growing range of projects. Today, NordLab brings together 3D modeling, printing, prototyping and custom design, with a focus on functionality, detail and finding the right solution for every project.
